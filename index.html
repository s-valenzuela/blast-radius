Blast Radius

An interactive map of your services and their dependencies. Click any service to see its blast radius: what it depends on, and what would break if it went down — both directly and transitively.

Using it

  • Click a service (in the list or the graph) to highlight its dependencies and everything impacted if it fails.
  • Graph / Matrix toggle to switch between the network view and a dependency matrix.
  • LB pools collapse services behind a shared load balancer; double-click a pool to expand it.
  • YAML menu — edit the graph inline, load a .yml file, or download the current one.
  • Your graph and node layout are saved in your browser, so a reload keeps your changes. Revert to default clears them.

Private & free

Everything runs in your browser — nothing is ever uploaded to a server. It's free and open source, released under the Apache 2.0 license.
